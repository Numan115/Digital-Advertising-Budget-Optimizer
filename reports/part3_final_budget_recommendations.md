## 2. Strategic Channel Rationale

### 1. Google (31.13% Allocation — Rs. 311,300)
- **Role:** High-intent direct conversion anchor.
- **Why:** Delivers the highest conversion rate (5.77%) and maximum ROAS (6.13x). Captures active commercial intent in major Pakistani metro areas (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad).

### 2. Instagram (30.54% Allocation — Rs. 305,400)
- **Role:** High-volume customer acquisition engine.
- **Why:** Delivers the highest total conversion volume (485 projected sales) at the lowest CPA (Rs. 630). Dominates the 18–34 demographic on mobile.

### 3. Facebook (20.33% Allocation — Rs. 203,300)
- **Role:** Retargeting and cross-generational retention.
- **Why:** Strong performance in mid-tier cities (Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan) with steady 4.64x ROAS.

### 4. TikTok (18.00% Allocation — Rs. 180,000)
- **Role:** Top-of-funnel customer discovery & viral reach.
- **Why:** Generates the highest click volume (12,047 clicks) at the lowest cost (Rs. 14.94 CPC).

### 5. YouTube (0.00% Allocation — Rs. 0)
- **Role:** Paused for direct-response performance ads.
- **Why:** High CPA (Rs. 901) and low ROAS (3.07x) make paid conversion ads inefficient compared to Google and Instagram. YouTube should be utilized for organic branding instead.
